Who We Are我们是谁

Our Mission我们的使命

The Church of God in SF Bay Area is a Christian community spread across the San Francisco Bay Area — from Berkeley to Stanford, Santa Clara and Cupertino. We gather to worship the Lord, pursue the truth of the Bible, and grow together in the divine life. Our desire is to experience and enjoy Christ in a caring church life, and to share His riches with the people around us. Every believer and every seeker is welcome to be part of God's household. 旧金山湾区教会是遍布湾区的一群基督徒——从伯克利到斯坦福、圣塔克拉拉和库比蒂诺。我们聚在一起敬拜主、追求圣经的真理，并在神圣的生命里一同长大。我们的心愿是在彼此相顾的教会生活中经历并享受基督，也把基督的丰富分享给身边的人。欢迎每一位信徒和慕道朋友来成为神家里的人。

We exist to glorify God by making disciples of Jesus Christ through faithfully speaking His Word, genuine worship, and loving community. We are a Christ-centered, Bible-believing church — many of us are students and young professionals — committed to sharing the gospel and serving our neighbors and campuses across the Bay. 我们存在是为了荣耀神：借着忠信地讲说神的话、真实的敬拜和相爱的群体，使人作耶稣基督的门徒。我们是以基督为中心、相信圣经的教会——其中很多是学生和年轻上班族——致力于传扬福音，服事湾区的邻舍和校园。

THE CHURCH教会
The church is a group of people who have been redeemed by the blood and regenerated by the Holy Spirit, who have given themselves into God's hands, and who are willing to take God's will, do His will, and stand for God on the earth to maintain His testimony. 教会是一班被血救赎、被圣灵重生的人，他们把自己交在神的手中，愿意接受神的旨意、遵行神的旨意，在地上为神站住，维持神的见证。

What We Believe我们的信仰

Our faith is grounded in the historic Christian faith and the unchanging truth of Scripture (Titus 1:4; Jude 3).我们的信仰建立在历代基督徒共同的信仰和永不改变的圣经真理上（多一4；犹3）。

01

The Bible as the Inspired Word of God圣经是神所默示的话

The Bible is the complete divine revelation, inspired word by word by God through the Holy Spirit, and is the ultimate guide for faith and life.圣经是神完整的启示，是神借着圣灵逐字默示的，是信仰和生活最终的准则。

2 Tim. 3:16提后三16
02

The Trinity三一神

One God who eternally exists in three persons — the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit — coexisting equally from eternity to eternity.独一的神，永远存在为父、子、圣灵三者，从永远到永远同时并存、同等。

Matt. 28:19; 2 Cor. 13:14太二八19；林后十三14
03

Salvation Through Jesus Christ借着耶稣基督得救

Salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone, who died for our sins, was buried, and rose bodily on the third day.得救是本乎恩、借着信，唯独信耶稣基督。祂为我们的罪死了，埋葬了，第三天身体复活。

Eph. 2:8; 1 Cor. 15:3-4弗二8；林前十五3～4
04

The Resurrection of Jesus耶稣的复活

Jesus Christ rose bodily from the dead, confirming His deity and the hope of eternal life for all who believe.耶稣基督身体从死里复活，证实祂是神，也给一切信的人永远生命的盼望。

Rom. 1:4; 1 Pet. 1:3罗一4；彼前一3
05

The Church as the Body of Christ教会是基督的身体

The church is the Body of Christ, called to worship together, grow in faith, and carry the gospel to every corner of the world.教会是基督的身体，蒙召一同敬拜、在信心里长大，并把福音带到地极。

Eph. 1:22-23; Col. 1:24弗一22～23；西一24

For Visitors给新朋友

Some questions people often ask. Don't see yours? Just contact us.大家常问的问题。没有找到你的问题？欢迎直接联系我们。

I'm not ready to come yet. Can someone contact me?我还没准备好来聚会，可以先有人联系我吗？
Of course! Coming to a meeting isn't the only way to get to know us. Leave your contact info through the contact form and we'll reach out in whatever way is most comfortable for you.当然可以！来聚会不是认识我们的唯一方式。通过联系表格留下你的联系方式，我们会用你觉得最舒服的方式联系你。
What happens at a meeting?聚会是什么样的？
Expect a warm welcome and joyful people. We sing to the Lord, read and share the Bible, pray together, and often share a meal. There's no fixed ritual — we simply want to worship the Lord in spirit and truthfulness (John 4:24).你会受到热情的欢迎，见到一群喜乐的人。我们一起向主唱诗、读经分享、祷告，常常也一起吃饭。没有固定的仪式——我们只是愿意在灵和真实里敬拜主（约四24）。
Do I have to be a Christian to come?一定要是基督徒才能来吗？
Not at all. Friends who are curious about the Bible or about Jesus are very welcome. Come with your questions!完全不用。对圣经或耶稣好奇的朋友都非常欢迎，带着你的问题来吧！
What language do you use?聚会用什么语言？
We are a bilingual church. Meetings use English and Chinese, and someone is always happy to help translate.我们是双语教会，聚会使用英文和中文，也总有人乐意帮忙翻译。
Is there a dress code?需要穿正装吗？
No dress code — come as you are.没有着装要求，穿得自在就好。
What denomination are you?你们属于哪个宗派？
We are not part of any denomination. We simply meet as Christians in the Bay Area and receive all believers, just as Christ has received us (Rom. 15:7).我们不属于任何宗派，只是在湾区单纯地以基督徒的身份聚会，接纳所有信徒，正如基督接纳了我们（罗十五7）。
